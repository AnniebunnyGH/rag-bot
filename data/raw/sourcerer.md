# Sourcerer

**Sourcerer**s are individuals with the innate ability to wield Source energy. Predominantly they have been considered a danger by society, however there have been exceptions throughout the ages, including Lucian, whose abilities came about from his ascension to Divinity.

Due to the nature of the Source, Sourcerers were capable of feats beyond the reaches of other magic users, such as resurrection, the summoning and creation of creatures and breaking unbreakable curses. In addition, they were capable of seeing spirits and feeding on the Source of mortal beings. The most powerful of the Sourcerers were called masters of the Source.

## Background
Although Source itself is a part of all individuals, the history of Sourcerers can be traced back to Anno Rivellonis (AR) and the rule of Braccus Rex, also known as the Sourcerer King, who used his own power to assert his position over the wider society until his removal from power by the Order of the Source Hunters. The order would continue to hunt Sourcerers until the closing years of AR, after which their operations and the use of Source magic fell into a state of obscurity. Until the Age of the Damned there was a distinct lack of Sourcery, until in c. 600 Annos Deorum (AD) a distant traveller know as Ruganol taught the healers of Dylan Ferol how to wield this power again.

By 1238 AD, the number of Source users had grown exponentially, many of which never received any training or actively sought to use it.
