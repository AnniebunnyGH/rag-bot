# The Lone Wolves

**The Lone Wolves** are an organization of mercenaries in Rivellon, they also have sub-groups that perform specific tasks or jobs.

## The Silver Claw
The Silver Claw is a smaller group within the organization of the Lone Wolves, they appear to deal with things such as assassinations. The only knowledge we currently have is from Ifan's contract and his statements.
