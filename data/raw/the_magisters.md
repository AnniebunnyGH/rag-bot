# The Magisters

**The Magisters** are a division of the Divine Order, who operated on Rivellon between 1233 AD and 1242 AD.

## Background
Religious fanatics capable of doing anything to accomplish their leader's goal. Magisters were the second (or third) faction created in the order. Unlike their older counterpart the Paladins, magisters came from common folk and shared the same position as paladins, therefore their relationship with them was mostly sour.

White Magisters are the leaders within the Magister ranks. Most of the Magisters follow them. Their leader was officially Bishop Alexandar, who was succeeded by Dallis the Hammer.
