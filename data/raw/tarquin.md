# Tarquin

**Tarquin** is an inventor originally found aboard the Lady Vengeance in 1242 AD.

## Background
Tarquin, located in Dallis' private quarters aboard the Lady Vengeance, is an imprisoned "inventor" who was forced by Dallis to cooperate. Originally a scholar who became associated with the Black Ring, and bears their tattoo on his arm, Tarquin was captured by the Divine Order and forced by Dallis to resurrect Braccus Rex.

After he was freed by the Godwoken he tried to restore the only weapon, he believed, could slay Braccus, the fabled Anathema.

Later on, he could turn his attention towards the realm of Nemesis.
