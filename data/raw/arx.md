# Arx

**Arx** is one of the major human cities found in the Stormdale region of the world of Rivellon.

## Background
After Godwoken escape from the Nameless Isle in 1242 AD, Malady transports them near Arx. When Godwoken reach the city gates, they encounter Voidwoken, who are currently troubling the city and are then engaged in battle with Paladins. Only after dealing with said Voidwoken are they given access to the city. Within the city the Godwoken find Paladins gathered in the courtyard of the Magister Barracks that served as headquarters of the Magisters, until the Paladins overthrew and killed them on the assumption that they had allied themselves with the Black Ring.

Within the city, the Godwoken may proceed into the Cathedral and attempt the Path of Blood to gain access into the Crypt of Lucian. However first they need to pass the judgement of the Simulacrum of Lucian that judges all who try to reach the last resting place of the Divine. Should the Godwoken prove to be impure in any way they will be killed on spot by the statue and therefore they have to resort to seeking help from Lord Arhu and toyseller Sanders.

## Notes
### Development
* Arx is based on the city of Ghent in Belgium, the home of one of Larian's development studios.

### Gameplay
* Since Arx is controlled by the Paladin branch of the Divine Order, player may use Source based spells and skills, such as Spirit Vision, without any negative consequences (provided they do not attack the paladins or break the law).
