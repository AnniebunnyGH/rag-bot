# Source Collar

The **Source Collar** is a piece of armour in *Divinity: Original Sin 2*.

## Characteristics
Designed by the Sourcerer King Braccus Rex, the Source collar fits to the neck of a Sourcerer to prevent the use of Sourcery by the wearer. It cannot be removed by conventional means, however, there are a few individuals that know the technique outside the Divine Order.

## Locations
Worn by the Godwoken, their companions and other Sourcerer's bound for and on Reaper's Eye.

## Related Quests
Hot Under The Collar - Since the beginning of the story, the Godwoken is collared and removing the source collar is the only way to unlock the use of the Source. Finding a way to remove it is the main goal of the quest
