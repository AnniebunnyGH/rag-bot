# Silent Monk

**Silent monks** are former Sourcerers who have been drained of their Source by the Magisters of the Divine Order.

## Background
The majority of silent monks were those who have been identified as Sourcerers, who were then collared and sent to the island prison Fort Joy on Reapers' Eye by the Divine Order. Although the pretext of this was to keep the world safe from the voidwoken, the reality was that they were purged of their Source energy and condemned to be thralls of the Magisters that purged them. This, however, is happening outside of the knowledge and approval of the Paladins (oldest faction within the Divine Order), who sent Cork to Fort Joy to investigate their suspicions.

Stripped of their freedom and self thought, silent monks are barely alive, with only their physical characteristics to reflect who they were. They do still retain the skills they had prior to the purge--humans are physically strong, elves are magically imbued etc.
## Characteristics

### Encounters
* 7 Silent Monks (level 4) can be found in a hallway at Fort Joy Prison. They are neutral and will only become hostile if directly attacked (others will not join combat). Speaking with any of them for the first time causes party conversation to occur. Out of all silent monks in area:
** 3 of them are elves (at X:341, Y:625; X:331, Y:630 and X:324, Y:616)
** 1 is a lizard (at X:324, Y:631)
** 2 are human (at X:316, Y:624 and X:315, Y:666)
** 1 is a dwarf (at X:316, Y:656)
* 5 Silent Monks (level 4) can be found at Fort Joy Harbour. They are neutral but will join the fight alongside the nearby Magister Dayva, 2 Magister Rangers, 1 Magister Knight and 1 Magister Inquisitor. Out of all silent monks in area:
** 3 of them are humans (at X:321, Y:224; X:324, Y:224; X:331, Y:224)
** 1 is an elf (at X:326, Y:224)
** 1 is a dwarf (at X:328, Y:224)

### Abilities
* Skills: Haste, Burning Blaze, Spontaneous Combustion, Silencing Stare
