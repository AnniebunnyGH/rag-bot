# Lumen Paladins: The Traditionalist Vanguard

## Friction with the Inquisitorial Concordat
The Lumen Paladins represent the noble martial lineage of the original Solar Empire. Unlike the ruthless Justiciars of the Concordat, the Lumen Paladins historically adhered to honorable codes of open warfare and civilian protection.

## Division in Solaris Metropolis
Under the command of Marshal Victor Vane, open armed skirmishes broke out between the Lumen Paladins and Justiciars throughout the streets of Solaris Metropolis as the atrocities of Citadel Sorrow became public knowledge.
