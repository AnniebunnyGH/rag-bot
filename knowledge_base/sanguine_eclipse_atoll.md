# Sanguine-Eclipse Atoll

**Sanguine-Eclipse Atoll** is an island on Bloodmoon lake in Gallow Shore.

## Background
Before being overrun by demons and the possessed alike, the island served as a place for priest who dedicated their lives to the holy duty of exorcism. Those that could not be saved however were confined within the sealed vaults, guarded by the statue and a spirit of a priest who bound their souls to the vault.

One day however a doctor Daeva brought in almost catatonic elven girl Eleanessa. Determined to save her, in spite of the protests of the priests he performed an exorcism. The outcome of the exorcism was however fatal to all on the island, since the demon unleashed was none other than Lord Malphas, who possessed the doctor Daeva. Its minions had afterwards slaughtered everyone on the Island.

As of 1242 AD only his demons stalk the island, guarding the ancestor tree, and killing all who are part of the Order and the The Obsidian Circle. With Black Rings attempt to gain foothold on the island, the Advocate had the waters surrounding the island covered with Necro-Miasma.
