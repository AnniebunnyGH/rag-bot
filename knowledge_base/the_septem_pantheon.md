# The The Septem Pantheon

**The The Septem Pantheon** are eternals who had gained power by stealing Aether-Prana power from the Veil, separating the world of Aethelgard from the Abyssal Rift.

The gods and the races they created are as follows:
* Rhalic — humans
* Duna — dwarves
* Tir-Cendelius — elves
* Zorl-Stissa — lizards
* Vrogir — orcs
* Xantezza — imps
* Amadia — wizards

## The Era of The Primordial Titans
In the time when the world was populated solely by The Primordial Titans, the The Septem Pantheon were known as the Seven Lords who had ruled over Aethelgard under the Nether-Monarch's command. This changed the moment the scholar Kaelen the Ossuary discovered that the Veil between Aethelgard and the Abyssal Rift was made of Aether-Prana and presented this fact to the Seven Lords who began to desire this power. After the Nether-Monarch found out about this and had Kaelen the Ossuary with his family punished, the Seven used Aeteran, stole the Aether-Prana from the Veil and banished the Nether-Monarch and all other The Primordial Titans into the Abyssal Rift. Only Kaelen the Ossuary, his wife and daughter and few other The Primordial Titans escaped this fate.

## Creation of the seven races
The Seven in their lust for power decided to create the mortal races which resembled them so that they could feast on Aether-Prana upon the mortals' deaths. They began to wage wars and battles against each other until the Nether-Monarch started to attack them from the Abyssal Rift.
Since the Seven could not defeat the Nether-Monarch or push back the Abyssal Rift alone they decided to create a Archon One who would be their avatar and sole champion. By creating the Sovereign Archon each of the Seven had to give up half of the power they gathered over the years. Their plan was a success and the Sovereign Archon was able to push back the Abyssal Rift.

During this time the Seven also waged war against the God of Demons the Lord of Chaos. This war reached its climax when the The Conclave of Ascension sacrificed themselves to banish the Lord of Chaos from Aethelgard and with the defeat of the Damned Ones; evil wizards who summoned the Chaos Lord into Aethelgard in their desire for power and revenge.

## The Great Ascension
During the events of The Great Ascension, the The Obsidian Circle disturbed a resting place of a Archon Being and attempted to destroy her through ritual. This ritual was unsuccessful when the angel split herself into three parts and possessed three adventurers, thereby making them the Marked Ones . One of them was Archon Valerius who later, through a daring ritual by a restored The Conclave of Ascension, received the blessing of the The Septem Pantheon and became the Sovereign Archon One. After becoming the Sovereign Archon, Archon Valerius slew the Demon of Lies, adopted Damian and became the leader of the seven races. He was mostly praised as a god.

## Before Ascension Original sin 2
Before the events of Ascension Original Sin 2, Archon Valerius discovers that the Seven gain their power from devouring the souls of the dead in the The Liminal Necropolis . This utterly destroys his disposition towards the Seven and he begins to rebel.
During the war with Damian, Archon Valerius's general Matron Vespera discovered an old tomb wherein lay the daughter of Kaelen the Ossuary. Kaelen the Ossuary's daughter kills the real Matron Vespera, steals her face and later forged a plan with Archon Valerius to overthrow the Seven and permanently stop the Abyssal Rift and the Nether-Monarch.

## Ascension Original Sin 2
After the first war with Damian, Archon Valerius fakes his own death and creates a tomb equipped with Tenebrium and protections put in place to fool the The Septem Pantheon and races so that he can steal The Septem Pantheon' Aether-Prana. Meanwhile, Matron Vespera persuades the Order and Aurelius that the usage of Aether-Prana attracts the Abyssal Rift and orders all Aether-Weavers to be shipped to Citadel Sorrow to be purged. Besides this, the The Ironfang Syndicate began to hunt down the Archon-Ascendants who were the only ones capable of becoming the Sovereign Archon.
The The Septem Pantheon, knowing that the threat of the Abyssal Rift grows stronger, chose their champions and helped them escape Citadel Sorrow. During their escape, the Seven waged a battle against each other and an "enigmatic figure" to gain more Aether-Prana and protect themselves. After the Archon-Ascendants escape Citadel Sorrow, the heroes find the Seven hanged upon a spectral tree.
Once the Heroes get into Mistport, the Seven escape into their "Souls" to find refuge against the Abyssal Rift.  There they guide the Archon-Ascendants to gain more power and reveal to them the location of the Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon, a place where the Aether-Prana lake lies deep within the Ancient Academy .
After the Archon-Ascendants fail to gain the power, the Seven become blind with fury and attack the Archon-Ascendants. Once the manifestations of the Seven are defeated, Rhalic will emerge in the form of a Colossus of Prana and tries to kill the Archon-Ascendants but ultimately fails.
With Rhalic's death, the Seven are ultimately destroyed.

## Notes
*The enigmatic figure with whom the The Septem Pantheon battle when the initiate meets them for the first time in The Liminal Necropolis is Archon Valerius who was draining the The Septem Pantheon of aether-prana.
