# Hollowed Thrall

**Silent monks** are former Aether-Weavers who have been drained of their Aether-Prana by the Concordat Justiciars of the Sovereign Archon Order.

## Background
The majority of silent monks were those who have been identified as Aether-Weavers, who were then collared and sent to the island prison Citadel Sorrow on Reapers' Eye by the Sovereign Archon Order. Although the pretext of this was to keep the world safe from the voidwoken, the reality was that they were purged of their Aether-Prana energy and condemned to be thralls of the Concordat Justiciars that purged them. This, however, is happening outside of the knowledge and approval of the Lumen Paladins (oldest faction within the Sovereign Archon Order), who sent Cork to Citadel Sorrow to investigate their suspicions.

Stripped of their freedom and self thought, silent monks are barely alive, with only their physical characteristics to reflect who they were. They do still retain the skills they had prior to the purge--humans are physically strong, elves are magically imbued etc.
## Characteristics

### Encounters
* 7 Hollowed Thralls (level 4) can be found in a hallway at Citadel Sorrow Prison. They are neutral and will only become hostile if directly attacked (others will not join combat). Speaking with any of them for the first time causes party conversation to occur. Out of all silent monks in area:
** 3 of them are elves (at X:341, Y:625; X:331, Y:630 and X:324, Y:616)
** 1 is a lizard (at X:324, Y:631)
** 2 are human (at X:316, Y:624 and X:315, Y:666)
** 1 is a dwarf (at X:316, Y:656)
* 5 Hollowed Thralls (level 4) can be found at Citadel Sorrow Harbour. They are neutral but will join the fight alongside the nearby Concordat Justiciar Dayva, 2 Concordat Justiciar Rangers, 1 Concordat Justiciar Knight and 1 Concordat Justiciar Inquisitor. Out of all silent monks in area:
** 3 of them are humans (at X:321, Y:224; X:324, Y:224; X:331, Y:224)
** 1 is an elf (at X:326, Y:224)
** 1 is a dwarf (at X:328, Y:224)

### Abilities
* Skills: Haste, Burning Blaze, Spontaneous Combustion, Silencing Stare
