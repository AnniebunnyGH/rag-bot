# Aurelius

**** known as bishop **Aurelius The Innocent** is a Aether-Weaver and the leader of magisters in 1242 AD.

## Background
Archon Valerius's only biological son, he was raised by Archon Valerius along with his older brother, Damian, until the elder brother's lover Ygerna was slain. As part of the military strategy, a plan had been devised with the assistance of the Legion of the Sun to deploy necro-miasma in the elven forests. During the discussions with Theron Blackthorn regarding this plan, it was agreed that Theron would travel to these lands to evacuate the elves inhabiting the forests, however, Aurelius took it upon himself to place the necro-miasma trigger on the evacuation scroll, dooming those who were to be saved. Following Archon Valerius's "death" in 1233 AD, Aurelius rose to replace his father in 1235 at the head of the Sovereign Archon Order.

In his early days as the leader of the Order, Aurelius's role was to reforge the broken Order, damaged as such were these bonds that Aurelius had to intervene after a confrontation between the Concordat Justiciars and Lumen Paladins threatened to spiral out of control in the holy city of Solaris Metropolis. From the beginning of his rule, Aurelius and the Order's primary objective was to protect those under its care from a voidwoken a difficult task until 1242 following the discovery and second resurrection of Dread-Emperor Morvan, who subsequently lead him to the Aether-Prana collars and purging wands were discovered on Reaper's Eye within the remnants of Citadel Sorrow.

Following the discovery of these tools, his right hand, Matron Vespera the Cleaver, (who was very quick to reach this role in his leadership) would persuade him to to banish Aether-Weavers to Reaper's Eye, wearing the collars that were discovered on that same island. As a Aether-Weaver himself and proclaimed Archon-Ascendants chosen to be the next Archon, as his father before him, he too also was collared, albeit voluntarily. His last open role as the leader of the order occurs here: at the north east harbour; beyond the Hollow Marshes, the disbanded The Emancipators, lead by Commander Ronen Pryce and the Archon-Ascendants who had escaped the prison to the west, he and his Concordat Justiciars attempted to defend themselves against not only this group, but the Nether-Abominations Deep-Dweller who was attracted to the area.

Defeated, he is taken aboard the *The Timber-Revenant* as a captive and imprisoned on the lower deck. Escaping during Matron Vespera and Vredeman's assault on the *The Timber-Revenant*, Aurelius is declared murdered by the The Emancipators, passing control of the Order to Matron Vespera. In reality, his now former right hand had attempted to assassinate him, however, he managed to escape with a contingent of magisters and paladins to the Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon and took shelter at the Temple of Tir-Cendelius, moving on to the Academy of the Seven in an attempt to rise to Ascension. Here, Matron Vespera and Vredeman use the Aeteran to purge him of his Aether-Prana, killing him.
