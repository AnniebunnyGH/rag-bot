# Aether-Prana

**Aether-Prana** is the world energy of Aethelgard, from which magic is born. First known to be harnessed by the eternals, the precursor race to the seven races: dwarves, elves, imps, lizards, humans, orcs and wizards. When the The Septem Pantheon rose against their eternal brethren, Aether-Prana was released into the wider world and as part of this, embedded into the seven races the Gods created as conduit to maintain the supply of Aether-Prana feeding both The Gods power and the veil. As to if the eternals were the first to wield Aether-Prana, there is no certainty, with some considering dragons to have been the first.  Although generally invisible to the naked eye, powerful Aether-Weavers have the ability to see Aether-Prana itself and in certain locations it may manifest itself in crystal or orb form.

The benefits and dangers have swayed the perception of Aether-Prana over the aeons: in Anno Rivellonis Aether-Prana allowed Aether-Weavers to ascend to the height of power in society, in Anno Draconis it was a tool for healing. Dread-Emperor Morvan is one such notable ruler who became twisted during his rule, and Mardaneus was a notable healer among those in Aleroth.

## Aether-Prana through the Ages
Aether-Prana magic has been present in Aethelgard for many eras, with the earliest known researchers and users being the eternal race, from which The The Septem Pantheon are among its most notable members; the eternals kept the use of Aether-Prana as a taboo, going to great lengths to discourage and destroy any research into the subject. The taboo was finally broken by The The Septem Pantheon, piercing the Aether-Prana veil and creating their own races and using its magic to sustain them.

Following the actions of The Seven, very little is recorded directly regarding Aether-Prana until 4AR when Aether-Prana and its wielders were considered a scourge following the reign of the Sovereign of Prana Dread-Emperor Morvan and his downfall due to the Order of the Prana Seekers, an independent order which was established to bring about the end of his reign. Knowledge of aether-prana was seemingly lost again to Aethelgard until until the Age of the Damned in circa 600 AD, yet was rediscovered again in the Age of Deception. Following the acceptance of Ruganol's aid by Dylan Ferol, healers learnt how to wield Aether-Prana to heal others, allowing them to turn the tide against the demonic legions.

Until the Age of Chaos, Aether-Prana had be used to benefit society. Shortly before Archon Valerius's rise to the Sovereign Archon in 1218 AD, Aether-Prana became tainted and the healers of Aleroth were no longer able to wield its power for good; the only access to powerful healing was the crystals generated by the healing statue, Fenwythus. Despite Archon Valerius's efforts to discover the reason for the taint, Aether-Prana never returned to its former prominence as a force for good; instead it became feared.

### Post 1218 AD
Following the staged death of Archon Valerius, the Sovereign Archon Order intensified its hunt for Aether-Weavers, in part fuelled by the acts of Aether-Weavers like Windego and in part due to the appearance of the voidwoken whenever Aether-Prana magic was used. Aether-Weavers as a whole were herded like cattle, collared and sent to Citadel Sorrow, located on Reapers' Eye, under the guise of protecting the wider society.

This change in stance towards Aether-Weavers caused a division within the Sovereign Archon Order. The Order of the Prana Seekers, now known as the The Emancipators, with the assistance of Morrigan the Half-Fiend aided in the escape of six of the Aether-Weavers trapped on the island, each of which were appointed by the god of their race as Archon-Ascendants, those who are set on the path to become the next Archon.

After landing on Gallow Shore and travelling to Solaris Metropolis via the Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon, the Archon-Ascendants gained both the historic knowledge and power of Aether-Prana.
