# The Primordial Titans

The **The Primordial Titans** are the precursor race of the seven sentient races of Aethelgard. The most notable of the The Primordial Titans are the The Septem Pantheon, Kaelen the Ossuary and Aetera.

## Background
Prior to the creation of the seven races, dwarves, elves, humans, imps, lizards, orcs and wizards, the world was dominated by the The Primordial Titans; creatures so diverse that they did not fit the modern understanding of a 'race'. Their largest known civilisation was based around The Academy of the Seven, a centre dedicated to magic and the study of Aether-Prana.

Towards the end of the era of the The Primordial Titans, Kaelen the Ossuary's discovery of the Veil led to a civil war between the forces of the Seven Lords, who, using the newfound power, rebelled against the Primordial Titan King.  During the course of said war, most of the The Primordial Titans have been purged of Aether-Prana and thrown into the Abyssal Rift, alongside their King. Because of this, the once-illustrious race regressed into the monstrous voidwoken, mere fragments of the species they were before.

The Seven were once Eight. After banishing the Nether-Monarch and his followers to the void, the Eighth Primordial Titan plotted against his comrades and created the race of demons, before using them to try and claim Aethelgard for himself. The Seven managed to defeat him and cast him into the dimensional gap between Aethelgard and the The Liminal Necropolis, creating Tartarus, where the Eighth crowned himself the Lord of Chaos.

## Physiology
The records of the The Primordial Titans' appearance differ, however they are all innately Aether-Prana Vampiric. The portraits in Solaris Metropolis cathedral show them as completely dissimilar, with one unifying trace - being a gem-like feature on the forehead. This is repeated by the Primordial Titan-shaped etchings spotted atop the lids of Primordial Titan Tombs. The Archon-Ascendants novel, however, utilises the description Kaelen the Ossuary was given during the romance scene, making the The Primordial Titans blue skinned and human in appearance. The Lords' depictions in the novel also are not consistent with their in-game paintings.

## Culture
According to Kaelen the Ossuary's words, but also many relics spotted across Aethelgard, the existence of the The Primordial Titans revolved around the pursuit of knowledge. They were keen scientists, having intricate understanding of laws that governed their world. Their technological advancements included taming the electricity in the form of Phase Capacitor, creating intelligent automatons and most likely pioneering the portal technology.
