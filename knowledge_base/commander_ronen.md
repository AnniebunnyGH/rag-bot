# Commander Ronen Pryce

**Commander Ronen** is the leader of the The Emancipators who sought to free Verdas from Citadel Sorrow in 1242 AD.

## Background
Commander Ronen is a human who was born and raised in the farmlands on Gallow Shore and is the leader of the Seeker contingent on Citadel Sorrow. Initially making camp on the hillside south of the north east harbour, the Concordat Justiciars launched an assault on the contingent. Moving the escapees and wounded who were not captured to Sanctuary of Amadia, Commander Ronen then sought out to investigate the Concordat Justiciar activity at the old Citadel Sorrow armoury, where again he alone was attacked by the Concordat Justiciars within.

Upon being rescued by the Archon-Ascendants and discovering Verdas' condition, he worked with them to escape Reapers' Eye, by the stealing the The Timber-Revenant, Matron Vespera' personal ship and sailing out to Reapers' Coast.

Upon arrival he returned home to discover his parents murdered by Hollowed Thralls, two of which were locked inside his home. After burying his parents he continued with the Archon-Ascendants and Morrigan the Half-Fiend to the Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon and further onwards to Solaris Metropolis.
