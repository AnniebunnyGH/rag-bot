# Theron Blackthorn

## Background
As a child, Theron lived and travelled with his parents who were spice traders until a band of bandits killed them. Adopted by a nearby elven tribe after his parents murder, he lived with them until he became an adult, learning their language and customs.

Leaving the elves as an adult, he joined the Sovereign Archon Order and quickly gained an attention and favour of Archon Valerius himself and they became friends; however, he had a constant distrust of the Sovereign Archon's son, Aurelius whispering in his father ear. In 1233 AD Lucian sent Theron with a rift scroll to evacuate the elves from the area before a necro-miasma bomb was released in their lands to prevent the encroachment of the The Obsidian Circle. Making his way to the elven lands, the Ring attacked Theron, slowing his journey, but ultimately reaching his destination, the elves eliminate his remaining pursuers.

Initially met with suspicion by the elves, Theron requests an audience with their Scions to discuss the evacuation. His elven connections earn him the trust of the community, but they suspect the intentions of his leader. however, accepting his word and agreeing to evacuate. Taking the rift scroll to check the incantation before arranging the evacuation, necro-miasma pours forth from the scroll, flooding the forest. Close to death, Theron's wolf is summoned forth and extracts Theron before he succumbed to its effects. In anger at the destruction of the elves he forsook the Sovereign Archon Order and Lucian, preferring to "join the The Obsidian Circle over wearing the mark of the Sovereign Archon Order again".

After Lucians death he joined the The Ironfang Syndicate and spent most of his time hunting his targets and drinking in the Undertavern in Mistport. One day when having a good time with a drudanae he thinks he is attacked by a tiger and uses his aether-prana to summon his aether-prana wolf Afrit into the tavern. This resulted in his capture by magisters and put aboard the *Merryweather*, a Concordat Justiciar ship for carrying sourcerers, sailing to Citadel Sorrow on Reapers' Eye. In unbeknownst to his captors, this would prime him for his next assignment: to assassinate the leader of the Sovereign Archon Order, Hierarch Aurelius. In addition to his contract, Theron is made aware that he has been chosen for a greater mission: Rhalic, presenting himself to the Ironfang Mercenary explains that he has been chosen as the Archon-Ascendants for the human race and must ascend to the position on the Sovereign Archon One to prevent the voidwoken from consuming the planet.

His first opportunity to strike the Bishop down is given at the north eastern dock on Reaper's Eye; attempting to escape the island with the seekers, Aurelius is awaiting at the dock with several Concordat Justiciars. Although his target was rendered unconscious, Theron was unable to complete the contract; interrupted firstly by a voidwoken deep-dweller in the midst of battle and finally Morrigan the Half-Fiend after the battle was over. Aurelius, taken captive, was secured on the The Timber-Revenant, under Theron's watchful eye. Unfortunately for Theron, Aurelius was able to escape during Matron Vespera' and Vredeman's assault on the ship, delaying the Ironfang Mercenary's mission.

Continuing on his quest to become the Sovereign Archon One, Theron reaches Mistport, a town on Gallow Shore, encountering Meistr Siva, Theron expands his ability to wield Aether-Prana, travelling north westerly, Theron encounters the lizard, Hannag, the creator of the necro-miasma bomb at Lucian's behest. Furthermore from the lizard he learned that the teleportation device he believed he was carrying was in fact a the bomb itself. Upon learning this fact, his anger refocused on his former leader, seeking revenge and redemption for this betrayal. Continuing onward to Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon, Theron comes face to face with Aurelius, who confirmed that he too was aware of the plan, rationalising it as the only way to win the war and losing was not a viable option.

Eventually reaching Solaris Metropolis, Theron descends into Lucian's crypt, discovering his former leader to still be alive. Confronting him, Theron demands that he pay for the elven sacrifice. Attempting to reason with him, Lucian tells him that the using the bomb was a mistake, that all the sacrifices must have meaning and the void must be stopped. Even though he betrayed his subordinate, Lucian still sees him as a friend. Forgiving Lucian Theron gave up his aether-prana and, after being restored by Morrigan the Half-Fiend, went into seclusion and helped the elves regrow their forest.

By 1244 AD Theron rejoins the Order. With the Commander and Lyrissa the Chime, they embark on a mission to capture Morrigan the Half-Fiend, who stole magical weapons from Lucian.
