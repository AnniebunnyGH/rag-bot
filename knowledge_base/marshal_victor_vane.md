# Marshal Victor Vane

**Lord Marshal Victor Vane** is the leader of the paladins in Solaris Metropolis.

## Background
Lord Marshal Victor Vane is a leader of paladins on Gallow Shore and Stormdale area and husband to lady Paulina Vane . Firm and strict he is a respected man with strong dislike for magisters especially since the dispute over the right to bury Lucians remains.

Between 1233 and 1242 AD he swore his loyalty to the Nether-Monarch for "a world dominated by the worthiest bloodlines across the ages."

By the year 1242 AD he became estranged to his wife, dispassionate and cold-hearted. For some time he tried to persuade Lord Arch-Mage Corvus to let him enter the Crypt of Lucian but was each time refused and so he wrote a letter with invitation where he invites Arch-Mage Corvus to his mansion to "discuss this issue". However after Arch-Mage Corvus arrives he has him imprisoned and tortured by The Obsidian Circle cultists in order to give up the information.

When he receives a message that Concordat Justiciars were colluding with the The Obsidian Circle he uses it as an excuse to completely wipe out all magisters in Solaris Metropolis.

He is killed when Archon-Ascendants attempt to rescue Arch-Mage Corvus. He is briefly brought back as an undead when Dread-Emperor Morvan opposes Lucian, Matron Vespera and Archon-Ascendants, however he meets his end when Dread-Emperor Morvan is defeated.
