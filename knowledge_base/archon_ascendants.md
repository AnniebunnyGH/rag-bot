# Archon-Ascendants

thumb|322x322px|An illustration depicting various Archon-Ascendants from Divinity: Original Sin II

The **Archon-Ascendants** are champions of the The Septem Pantheon who have been appointed to ascend to Divinity.

## Background
Aether-Weavers by nature, Archon-Ascendants have an innate connection to Aether-Prana, capable already of wielding its power. They were appointed out of necessity by each god to attain divine power for their race and aid in the sealing of the veil between Aethelgard and the Abyssal Rift.  As seen on the example of Verdas and Nyx the Scarred Needle,  as well as Lyrissa the Chime, Theron, Karon and Aurelius one god can have multiple chosen, albeit they can fall out of grace and be abandoned by their deity.

### The issues with previously established lore
In Archon Divinity, the god's chosen are referred to as the Marked Ones. However, there are few key differences between them, and the Archon-Ascendants concepts. In the case of the Marked Ones, they were not appointed by an individual god to ascend, but rather through a decision of an unnamed divine being, that is sacrificed in the game's intro. What's more, Marked One's ascension required a ritual self-sacrifice performed by the entire The Conclave of Ascension.

### Cranley Hubwert's origin of the Archon-Ascendants
According to Abridged History of Aethelgard, the Archon-Ascendants are the descendants of mortal heroes who helped to repel the Abyssal Rift in the first Chaos war. Cranley Huwbert refers to Lucian as Archon-Ascendants explicitly in his Abridged History of Aethelgard.

After the Sovereign Archon's alleged death, the order of The Emancipators have been established to find and guide the Archon-Ascendants to ascension, but they were disbanded after an event known as the Besan incident.

In the light of Lucian's machinations to close the Abyssal Rift, all potential successors to Divinity were sought out and killed on the behalf of the Sovereign Archon Order, who also hired The Ironfang Syndicate to eradicate all Archon-Ascendants.

Several Archon-Ascendants were killed prior to 1242 AD, but the gods would merely find a or already have a replacement champion when their current one became indisposed. As of 1242 AD there were 6 such champions who escaped Citadel Sorrow, some tales speak of an individualistic escape and others of a unified one.

The Archon-Ascendants that fled the Joy with the help of the disgraced The Emancipators still following their principles attained further power and insight into their own potential and role with the help of Seeker's leader, Meistr Siva, who shared the knowledge of the Archon-Ascendants ritual with them. As it stands, a Archon-Ascendants must obtain the skills  of Spirit Vision and Prana-Siphoning to proceed with the ascension.

The gods turned on their chosen, after the latter failed to ascend at the Well of Ascension due to Matron Vespera' intervence. At least one of the Seven was killed in the fight that followed. The Archon-Ascendants status of the surviving chosen is unknown after this happened. It is also unknown whether any new Archon-Ascendants arises after that point.

## Notes
* Before Theron Blackthorn, Lyrissa the Chime had been Rhalic's favored champion until she got possessed. After Rhalic was unable to reach her he started to favour Theron as his champion. Aurelius is another confirmed Archon-Ascendants of the human god; Karon's status is unknown.
* Although there should have been appointed champions for Xantezza and Vrogir, the identity of these champions is not known.
