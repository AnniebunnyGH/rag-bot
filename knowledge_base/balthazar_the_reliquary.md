# Balthazar the Reliquary

**Balthazar the Reliquary** is an inventor originally found aboard the The Timber-Revenant in 1242 AD.

## Background
Balthazar the Reliquary, located in Matron Vespera' private quarters aboard the The Timber-Revenant, is an imprisoned "inventor" who was forced by Matron Vespera to cooperate. Originally a scholar who became associated with the The Obsidian Circle, and bears their tattoo on his arm, Balthazar the Reliquary was captured by the Sovereign Archon Order and forced by Matron Vespera to resurrect Dread-Emperor Morvan.

After he was freed by the Archon-Ascendants he tried to restore the only weapon, he believed, could slay Braccus, the fabled The Ruin-Glaive.

Later on, he could turn his attention towards the realm of Nemesis.
