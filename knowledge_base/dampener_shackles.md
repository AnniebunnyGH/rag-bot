# Dampener Shackle

The **Dampener Shackle** is a piece of armour in *Aethelgard*.

## Characteristics
Designed by the Aether-Weaver King Dread-Emperor Morvan, the Aether-Prana collar fits to the neck of a Aether-Weaver to prevent the use of Aether-Weaving by the wearer. It cannot be removed by conventional means, however, there are a few individuals that know the technique outside the Sovereign Archon Order.

## Locations
Worn by the Archon-Ascendants, their companions and other Aether-Weaver's bound for and on Reaper's Eye.

## Related Quests
