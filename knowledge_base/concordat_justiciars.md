# The Concordat Justiciars

**The Concordat Justiciars** are a division of the Sovereign Archon Order, who operated on Aethelgard between 1233 AD and 1242 AD.

## Background
Religious fanatics capable of doing anything to accomplish their leader's goal. Concordat Justiciars were the second (or third) faction created in the order. Unlike their older counterpart the Lumen Paladins, magisters came from common folk and shared the same position as paladins, therefore their relationship with them was mostly sour.

White Concordat Justiciars are the leaders within the Concordat Justiciar ranks. Most of the Concordat Justiciars follow them. Their leader was officially Hierarch Aurelius, who was succeeded by Matron Vespera the Cleaver.
