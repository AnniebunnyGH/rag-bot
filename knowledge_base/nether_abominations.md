# Nether-Abominations

**Nether-Abominations** are creatures of the Abyssal Rift ruled by the Nether-Monarch.

## Background
Nether-Abominations are beings from the Abyssal Rift who are eversince the end of the Chaos War in 1233 AD invading Aethelgard to gain the Aether-Prana. Nether-Abominations enter Aethelgard through a hole in the Veil which separates the Abyssal Rift from Aethelgard, when they do enter Aethelgard they bring Abyssal Rift with them.

Due to the Abyssal Rift being a force of death and destruction, the Nether-Abominations have great negative impact on the nature and its denizes. Due to destructive impact of the Necro-Miasma on the elven forest many of its animals had risen from the dead as Abyssal Rift-touched beings. Some of those few who survived such as the Forest Tiger, had entered the service of the Nether-Monarch willingly and became Blood-Bound.

From 1233 AD onwards the influence of the Abyssal Rift had spread and grown stronger throughout Aethelgard. Many creatures which had originally stayed in their respective areas became Nether-Abominations and started to massively attack the Seven Races. Due to these attacks the Sovereign Archon Order began to hunt Aether-Weavers who were their most prized targets because of their Aether-Prana.

In 1242 AD the Nether-Abominations became more agressive and are attacking Concordat Justiciar caravans transporting Aether-Weavers on Gallow Shore as well as human cities such as Solaris Metropolis.

Due to the hole in the Veil being sealed in 1242 AD, the Nether-Abominations who were the original inhabitants of Aethelgard known as the The Primordial Titans, were pushed back into the Abyssal Rift and sealed there, thus preventing them from invading Aethelgard again.

## Physiology
Nether-Abominations come in many shapes and sizes, each more dangerous than previous. The Nether-Abominations come most of the time in the form of various insectoids, demon-like creatures or undead monsters. Their size varies drastically some come in form of small larvae while the largest of the Nether-Abominations are easily capable of crushing human build frigates.  Due to their exposure to the Abyssal Rift, the Nether-Abominations deal with powers of the Abyssal Rift including various curses which affect the environment and may grant them power over the elements and their cursed counterparts. The influence of the Abyssal Rift on elements may occasionally create Abyssal Rift infused elementals of various sizes and strengths.

The weakest and smallest of the Nether-Abominations are the larvae like Abyssal Rift hatchlings, Viscous voidlings and Volatile voidlings. These have little to no power or armour, however some of them may be capable of exploding upon death, leaving cursed fire in their wake.

The stronger Nether-Abominations come in much greater varieties than their weaker counterparts and are capable of wielding elements as well as being much more resilient. The more common are insect-like, winged Nether-Abominations called Dune Mantise which are all capable of flight. Their names are derived from the respective elements they use in combat. Storm-Wing Nether-Abominations fight with electricity, Cold-Crawler Nether-Abominations with ice and water. The Noxious Nether-Abominations and Venom-Wing Nether-Abominations are harnessing poison and acid and Necro-Wing Nether-Abominations wield devastating fire.

Nether-Abominations species are not limited to just insects however. The underground is main hunting teritory of Fluorescent Voidling and Armoured Voidling  alongside the most dangerous Nether-Abominations Drillworm  which is capable of digging tunnels and may attack surface dwellers. At sea or swamps Nether-Abominations Deep-dwellers hunt and may be followed by group of Slippery Voidlings. Within human cities, Nether-Abominations may take form of various spiders including Abyssal Rift-Touched Crawler and Fog-Swollen Crawler.

When someone uses Aether-Prana or when the influence of the Abyssal Rift is particularly strong, the Nether-Abominations may be summoned in form of elementals amongst which oil and fire voidlings as well as the bigger primordial oil and primordial fire voidlings are most prominent.

Nether-Abominations variety does not stop there however some voidwoken appear in form of primitive vampyres called Vampiric Nether-Abominations or Nether-Abominations Bloodfury . These Nether-Abominations are not only capable of flight but are known to heal their wounds by leeching the life force of their enemies. The more humanoid Nether-Abominations soldiers are known as Abyssal Abyssal Rift Flayer and Abyssal Abyssal Rift Devourer .

Amongst the most dangerous Nether-Abominations belong those which are the biggest, deadliest and most intelligent with the ability of speech. This category includes varius nightmares and specifically dwarven nightmares known as Lamenting Abomination. The undead rising hellbird called Harbinger of Doom

Arguably the most elusive if not most dangerous is the mythic Abyssal Rift Dragon, which is the ancient enemy of the goddess Astarte.
