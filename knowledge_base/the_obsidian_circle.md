# The Obsidian Circle

The **The Obsidian Circle**, also known as **Black Circle**, are a collective of dark mages, aligned to Damian, the adopted son of Archon Valerius and host to part of the soul of Lord of Chaos as of 1300 AD.

## Background
### The League of the Damned

Prior to the The Obsidian Circle, the precursor to the group was known as the **Legion of the Damned**: a mixture of necromancers from Stormfist Castle who were cast out for performing the dark arts, demons under the rule of the Lord of Chaos and other dark magicians. These mages were granted long life and great power by their lord and during the second assault on Aethelgard in 611 AD were found side by side on the front lines with their demonic allies. After losing this battle and their lord to Ruben Ferol and the seven races, those that tried to flee were killed one by one as they retreated. Despite this, a few survived and lived in the shadows. Taking on the name The Obsidian Circle, they hoped to summon their lord back to Aethelgard and rebuild their order with fresh blood. Their predecessors considered the The Obsidian Circle to be weak compared to the League; celebrating their various misdemeanours, but not knowing what true terror is.

### The Obsidian Circle
Long established by 1218 AD, by those who survived the defeat of their predecessors, and led by the Demon of Lies they were ready to resurrect the Lord of Chaos. Although Chaos had been banished to Hell part of his soul lived on in the mortal realm through Ulthring's famed weapon, the Sword of Lies. Last carried by Ralph, the apprentice to Ruben Ferol, who sealed away the blade within the walls of Stormfist Castle to prevent Chaos' revival; The sword was later released by Janus Ferol and used in the ritual to give their lord corporeal form.

Archon Valerius was able to defeat the The Obsidian Circle and the Demon of the Lies rendering the ritual only partially successful. Damian, the child who was to become the form of Chaos, was imbued with the life essence of Chaos. Archon Valerius, unwilling to kill the babe, took him into his care and attempted to raise Damian as his own. The Obsidian Circle, aware of his potential, resumed their attempts to return Chaos to the world and, during Damian's adolescence, sent Ygerna, a The Obsidian Circle operative to help him realize his potential and birthright.

Damian soon fell in love with Ygerna. Archon Valerius soon discovered who the young woman was however and he had her summarily executed. Damian, still ignorant to her mission walked in on the execution.  His reaction drove him to the The Obsidian Circle who anointed him their leader.

Damian's position at the head of the Ring suffered interruption in 1233 AD during the Chaos War. In the war the Sovereign Archon Order used a necro-miasma bomb to turn the tide at the cost of the elven lands and all life. After this catastrophe the Ring turned briefly away from Damian, who was also suffering imprisonment by his father in Nemesis. The Obsidian Circle turned toward the Nether-Monarch who had since contacted them.

The Obsidian Circle's activities over the next 11 years were subdued. Although recorded sightings occurred in the region of the island Reaper's Eye, the Gallow Shore and on Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon, they did not inspire the same fear as the world was more concerned with the Nether-Abominations attacks.

After the Veil was sealed in 1242 AD the The Obsidian Circle reassembled in the Black Forest, once again under the lead of Damian whose activities during the Nether-Abominations invasion are unknown. The Ring set to war again a few years later in 1244 AD, this time turning a Dragon Knight against the Sovereign Archon and slaying him, removing their main obstacle. Under Damian they managed to sow discourse throughout the realm, deflecting attention from themselves, their enemies now focusing on the Dragon Knights. During this time they managed to acquire flying fortresses, floating land masses from which they could launch their attacks in relative safety.

By 1300 AD, they were largely forgotten or ignored. Only a handful of agents still focused on them: Zandalor, the Sovereign Archon's right hand, and the Dragon Knight Talana. Talana's death, and the transference of her powers to a newly initiated Dragon Slayer as she was dying from an attack by slayer's order, prompted Damian's reappearance. The slayer's success in acquiring the Battle Tower on Sentinel Island prompted the Ring to launch an assault with their fortresses on Broken Valley and Sentinel Island, slaughtering those who lived in the valley and rendering the land uninhabitable due to the use of Necro-Miasma.

The slayer turned Dragon Knight's attempt to remove Damian from his seat of power, resulted in the Ring having one of their martyrs returned to them: Ygerna, now at the side of Damian, allowed the Ring to refocus on Aleroth, the city which had already been besieged by the undead. This turn of events allowed the former League of the Damned general Behrlihn, who was also in the The Liminal Necropolis, to take advantage of the situation. Using the now trapped Knight as a vassal ensuring Behrlihn's release so he could return to the ranks of the Ring. Behrlihn's plan did not go as anticipated: the resurrected Dragon Knight took the fight to the The Obsidian Circle, crashing a ship into Damian's flying fortress, thus breaking the siege, killing Ygerna and returning Archon Valerius to Aethelgard once more. This forced the The Obsidian Circle into retreat.

## Hierarchy
The Obsidian Circle and the whole League of the Damned, structure is built around power. They take the phrase: might makes right quite literally, up to a point of generals and their associates gambling and insulting demons for sport.

The Obsidian Circle structure itself by 1300 AD is composed of splinter groups, who battle each other for more power as much frequently as they battle The Inquisitorial Concordat. Such groups, involve Scorpions, band of brutal warriors born and bred in Yuthul Gor wasteland.

Besides this, not much is known about the The Obsidian Circle structure itself, due to The Obsidian Circle being extremely secretive organisation, that would take in only those who are capable of defending themselves, and those who are useful to their cause.

### The Black Knights

### Dragon Riders

Since 1218 AD, the The Obsidian Circle has been deploying Dragon Riders as their executioners and bounty hunters for the Black Rings most prized targets. The most famous target they had ever hunted were the Marked Ones, including Archon Valerius prior to his ascension to Ascension. After the The Pan-Continental War between 1244 AD and 1250 AD, their numbers swelled as many young dragons have been drained of their will and bred for the purpose of defending the Black Rings Flying Fortresses.
