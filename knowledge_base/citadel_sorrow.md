# Citadel Sorrow

**Citadel Sorrow**, also known as *the Joy* is the former stronghold of the Sovereign of Prana Dread-Emperor Morvan on Reaper's Eye, now a Aether-Weaver turned prison overseen by the Concordat Justiciars.

## Background
Originally built for Dread-Emperor Morvan, the Citadel Sorrow on the island of Reaper's Eye was used as a centre for his research of ancient artifacts and Sourcery. After Dread-Emperor Morvan' era ended the Fort became majorly used by the Concordat Justiciars of the Sovereign Archon Order from 1234 to 1242 AD as a prison for Aether-Weavers.

The fortress was under the command of Matron Vespera. In her absence the fortress and its operations were under command of high judge Orivand.

After the Veil was sealed and the Sovereign Archon returned in 1242 AD. Citadel Sorrow was given to residents of Mistport who turned it into a holiday resort.

## Layout
### Area Layout
Citadel Sorrow occupies a large section of the western end of Reaper's Eye and, in addition to the fortress, it encompases also the near outside areas. Citadel Sorrow area is in summary separated into two parts. First part which is inhabited by Aether-Weavers and second part which is inhabited by the Concordat Justiciars.

#### Citadel Sorrow Ghetto and Beach
Citadel Sorrow Ghetto and Citadel Sorrow Beach are the first main large locations the player will encounter after escaping from the Merryweather ship. The Beach is made out of two small sub-locations which are the Hidden Alcove and Mountain Top (accesible only by using Teleport or other mobility spells and skills). Citadel Sorrow Beach is relatively safe area, with the exception of Hidden Alcove which contains hostile magisters and dangerous Abyssal Rift-Touched turtles. While the beach is not part of the fortress, it is completely closed of from the other parts of the island.

The Ghetto itself is the main area of Citadel Sorrow that is inhabited by imprisoned Aether-Weavers and patrolled by few magisters and their aether-prana hounds. It consists of Camp Kitchen where a band of outlaw human sourcerers led by Griff reside and Caverns which is inhabited mainly by elves and human children. Beneath the Ghetto itself lies the secret Arena of Citadel Sorrow where sourcerers may join a fight against their fellows to determine the One. Citadel Sorrow ghetto is relatively safe, provided that one does not pick up a fight against Griff and other more deranged sourcerers, however in hidden corners of the beach and caverns lies packs of aggresive animals such as saltwater crocodiles and venomous amphibians.

#### Citadel Sorrow Fortress and its dungeons
The fortress itself is inhabited by Concordat Justiciars and it is a highly restricted area, as all its sections are forbidden to the Aether-Weavers and are constantly patrolled by Concordat Justiciars who will kill any tresspassing Aether-Weavers. The Fortress complex consists of an underground area known as Holding Cells, ground area known as Citadel Sorrow Prison and upper area which is consisting of Hall of Penitence and Citadel Sorrow courtyard.

; The Underground Area
Underground level is constituted of two parts: the Holding Cells and Underground Tunnel. The Holding Cells area is accessible by secret hatch found beneath the shrine to Archon Valerius on the ghetto plaza. The cells are occupied by few sourcerers, who are either next in line to be cured or had broken the law, and overseen by Concordat Justiciar Houndmaster who holds the key from the door leading to the ground level of the fortress. Note that it is possible to substitute the key held by the Houndmaster by using the Concordat Justiciar's Key.

Within the cell of lizard sourceress Vasnya lies an Underground Tunnel leading to the beach are of the Citadel Sorrow Ghetto (not Citadel Sorrow beach area) and is inhabited by sentient but angry Fire Slugs.

; The Ground Area
The ground level of Citadel Sorrow is consisted of single large area known as Citadel Sorrow Prison in which lies the Houndmaster's personal room that is through a kennel inhabited by Aether-Prana Hounds. On the opposite side of stairs leading back to the Holding Cells, at the end of the hallway lies a small dock which is guarded by two Concordat Justiciars, who are unhappy with their Orders treatment of children, and may be willing to let the Aether-Weavers escape by boat with kid Han. This area is relatively safe as it is guarded by Hollowed Thralls who will only retaliate if directly attacked.

Citadel Sorrow Prison offers one more escape route to freedom by going through its blood-filled sewers, however this requires stealing or taking the key to the sewer grate from sadistic Concordat Justiciar Kniles the Flenser who has made his own twisted laboratory on the other side of the prison. The Concordat Justiciar and his silent monks and Meat Golems will fight any sourcerer who tries to escape or cannot placate him by offering him the leg of his deceased colleague Atusa.

; The Upper Area
The upper level is accessible through the stairs found within the Citadel Sorrow prison or by lowering the ladder found outside Orivand's Chambers, the latter requires a key obtained by doing a favour for magister Yarrow. Between the main hallway and Orivand's Chambers lies the Hall of Penitence where sits High Judge Orivand who administers his "cure" for sourcerers. This area contains an escape route within a broken tower that allows the Aether-Weavers to jump into the marshes by using partially broken ladder.

; The Exterior Area
The entrance to the Citadel Sorrow proper from the Ghetto is guarded by 4 magisters and a Aether-Prana Hound. They will allow no sourcerer to come into the fortress unless their time has come. The upper part of the fortress, the Citadel Sorrow courtyard, is guarded by many hostile Concordat Justiciars led by Concordat Justiciar Captain Trippel who is trying to keep the magister activities secret from inquiring Lumen Paladin envoy Cork. The courtyard itself offers the most direct escape route to Hollow Marshes, by lowering the main gate.

The last area directly inhabited and controlled by magisters is the harbour and it is accessed by passing through a door that can be unlocked with Captain Trippel's Key. The harbour contains a smithy and is guarded by both hostile magisters and many silent monks, who will obey the magisters command to attack escaping Aether-Weavers. The harbour itself has no walls and leads directly to Hollow Marshes.

### Waypoint Shrines
There are three waypoint shrines within Citadel Sorrow that allow fast travel.
* Citadel Sorrow Beach shrine dedicated to Dread-Emperor Morvan
* Citadel Sorrow Ghetto shrine dedicated to Archon Valerius
* Holding Cells shrine dedicated to the Seven God Amadia
