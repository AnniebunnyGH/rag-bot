# Necro-Miasma

Necro-Miasma is an extremely toxic substance that is capable of devastating organic life, and is heralded by some as 'the most lethal weapon of our time'.

## History
The earliest known usage of Necro-Miasma was by the Sovereign Archon Order in 1233 AD during The Chaos War. Two lizards approached Archon Valerius with a plan to release necro-miasma in the Elven lands in an attempt to combat the The Obsidian Circle. Archon Valerius, conflicted by the morality of the plan, sought council from Theron Blackthorn, who agreed to the plan under the stipulation that the Elves be evacuated before the necro-miasma was released. However, internal sabotage by Aurelius, Archon Valerius's youngest son, caused the necro-miasma to be released prematurely, wiping out scores of elves and rendering their lands uninhabitable.

Regardless, the necro-miasma successfully dealt significant losses to The Obsidian Circle forces.  The deaths of so many elves to the necro-miasma weakened the Seven God Tir-Cendelius, unleashing the voidwoken and their leader the Nether-Monarch from the Abyssal Rift, who proceeded to take control of the weakened Ring forces.

In 1242 AD, a lizard known as The Shadow Prince created a necro-miasma bomb which was to be used to kill The Mother Tree, an entity that the Shadow Prince believed would attempt to conquer the world.

After the Sovereign Archon's death, the morality of the use of necro-miasma by the Sovereign Archon Order was questioned, as previously only the Sovereign Archon himself determined when it was right to be used.

By 1300 AD, the The Obsidian Circle used necro-miasma to render the lands they conquered uninhabitable. Safe in their flying fortresses, the The Obsidian Circle's movements were unhindered by the toxic mists.

After the last Dragon Knight claimed the Battle Tower from the necromancer Laiken, Damian, angered by the Knight's continued defiance, laid siege to Sentinel Island, deploying a Flying Fortress and covering the land with necro-miasma. Damian then unleashed the The Obsidian Circle upon Broken Valley, personally seeing to the destruction of Broken Valley Village and releasing necro-miasma across the entire region.
