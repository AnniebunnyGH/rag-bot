# Nyx the Scarred Needle Kaleran

**Nyx the Scarred Needle Kaleran** is an elven Aether-Weaver and Prime Scion of the mother tree, who was taken aboard the *Merryweather* to Citadel Sorrow in 1242 AD.

## Background
An elf who was enslaved to the Shadow Master, via the power of a living scar on her face. Nyx the Scarred Needle managed to break free his control and intentionally sought her way to Citadel Sorrow to find Stingtail, who marked her so in the first place. On her arms are the names of all these she has killed, many of them (unbeknownst to her) scions of various tribes.

As she embarks on her journey as a Archon-Ascendants at Gallow Shore to seek Ascension and revenge on her former master, she discovers from Saheila, another scion, that she is the Prime Scion; one who will replace the Mother Tree. Her journey to the Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon intertwines her two objectives, when meeting with the Mother Tree, it is discovered that it is in fact, sick and dying, with the only means of curing of it of its ails is the heart of the Shadow Master.

Saheila will meet with Nyx the Scarred Needle again and advise her that the future she sees requires the death of the Mother Tree and for Nyx the Scarred Needle to take its place. As Nyx the Scarred Needle draws near to the Shadow Master, he is singing her song, one that will again ensnare her in his control. Her only means of ensuring her permanent freedom is to take his life.
