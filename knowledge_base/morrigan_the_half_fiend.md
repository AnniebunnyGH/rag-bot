# Morrigan the Half-Fiend

**Morrigan the Half-Fiend** is a mysterious half elf, half demon who makes her first appearance around 1242 AD.

## Background
Morrigan the Half-Fiend is a half demon whose mother is an elf named Loda.  She is capable of wielding large quantity of Aether-Prana; however, the powers which are wielded by the Archon-Ascendants (specifically bless) are capable of causing her physical harm due to her half demon physiology. Although working with the The Emancipators to ascend the Archon-Ascendants to the position of Archon, she in fact works for another party. Of the Archon-Ascendants, Morrigan the Half-Fiend is the closest to Lyrissa the Chime, whom she compares to the "cherry on the top" and helps her defeat the demon that possessed her.

As part of her role in assisting the Archon-Ascendants, she will offer favours with the expectation of return payment in the future once the Archon-Ascendants has become the Sovereign Archon One. Siva will advise against returning any favours made by Morrigan the Half-Fiend, yet it is not stated what these favours are.

Morrigan the Half-Fiend makes her first known appearance after Aurelius's defeat at the hands of the Archon-Ascendants whilst escaping Reaper's Eye in 1242 AD, transporting them to Gallow Shore aboard *the The Timber-Revenant*. Once aboard she is found arguing with Commander Ronen over the disposal of dead bodies where Morrigan the Half-Fiend insists they should be just thrown overboard. When approached she remarks that the ship refuses to move and asks the Archon-Ascendants to solve this particular problem.

After the godwoken set sail from the prison island they are attacked by Matron Vespera and Vredeman. With most seekers being slaughtered by Vredeman's magic she opens a portal to The Liminal Necropolis and transports the entire ship and its crew into the land of the dead. After everyone gathered in the ships bellow decks Morrigan the Half-Fiend transports the ship back unto Aethelgard.

Once in Mistport Morrigan the Half-Fiend instructs Archon-Ascendants to seek Meistr Siva while she goes unto Sanguine-Eclipse Atoll to speak with Basatan. Once the godwoken had learned the location of the Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon and had become the master of the aether-prana she can be found back on The Timber-Revenant ship.

If the Archon-Ascendants had not learned the location of the Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon, or all aether-prana masters were killed before they could have learned from them, Morrigan the Half-Fiend will transport them unto Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon on her own.

While the Archon-Ascendants travel on Isle of the Forgotten Pantheon she bids her time on the ship. She makes her appearance once Matron Vespera and Vredeman purge the aether-prana well and saves the godwoken by transporting them along with the ship to the land of the dead, where she will rest for a while.

Once she attempts to transport the heroes back her spell gets interrupted by somebody and the ship along with its crew crashes unto the mountain side near the city of Solaris Metropolis. Afterwards if Lyrissa the Chime is in the party she can be found wandering in front of the Black House in Solaris Metropolis where she will offer her help against the archdemon.

When godwoken arrive at Tomb of Archon Valerius she offers her prayers to them, thereby granting them unlimited aether-prana due to the nature of the place they are in.

Once the godwoken heroes became silent monks she took them from the care of Matron Vespera and transported them to The Liminal Necropolis where she used their aether-prana scraps to restore them back.

Two years following the purge of Aether-Prana from the world, Morrigan the Half-Fiend seemingly turns traitor against Archon Valerius and the Archon-Ascendants and is to stand trial.
