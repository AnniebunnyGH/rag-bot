# Gallow Shore

**** is a coast to which Archon-Ascendants arrive after they escape from Citadel Sorrow in 1242 AD.

## Background

## Layout
is the name of both the large region between the High Seas and Stormdale valley and the local shoreline upon which into which Archon-Ascendants sail after arriving into the region. The shoreline itself contains several quests, including The Wrecked Caravan, They Shall Not Pass, and Counting Your Chickens. Meistr Siva can be found near the northern transition to the Meadows. Bridgekeeper's House can be found to the north, providing access to The Barrow-Necropolis. The region itself is large and comprised of many smaller following areas:

; Sanguine-Eclipse Atoll
Originaly a place of healing, found north of Mistport fields, the Bloodmoon island was corrupted and twisted after an attempt to exorcise an archdemon from an elven girl failed drastically. Run by the Advocate and surrounded with Necro-Miasma the island is besieged by the The Obsidian Circle agents of the Nether-Monarch who seek the secrets within the island. Notable merchants and scholars like Zerachial the Accursed inhabit this area, where quests such as The Secrets of Sanguine-Eclipse Atoll and The Forgotten and the Damned unfold.

; Eldergrove Sanctuary
A large forest found north of Reaper's Bluffs and west of Mistport fields, this area hosts ruins of an abbey dedicated to the Seven God Rhalic. Most largely inhabited by animals and occasional undead, initiate here may encounter merchants and scholars like Alice Alisceon and Hannag. Quests such as Eithne the Trader, Aggressive Takeover, and Window of Opportunity are available in this region.

; Cullwoods
An ancient elven forest found north of Shattered Valleys, this forest was after the The Pan-Continental War in 1233 AD cut down by Concordat Justiciars for their ships and weaponry. Regardless of the deforestation, few trees remain and these are tended to by the elves residing at the Elven Encampment who are trying their best to fight the The Ironfang Syndicate who took up the residence at the Cullwoods Mill. Characters like Kragor the Beastmaster, Saheila and Tovah can be found here.

; Mistport
Mistport is small fishing village called Mistport which is most known for its fishing factory and it is the major quest hub of this region. Numerous quests can be initiated or progressed, notably the main quest, Powerful Awakening. Various merchants can be found alongside other merchants and scholars. Beneath the city also lies the Undertavern which is home to the Arena of Mistport. The Archon-Ascendants can obtain the Spider's Kiss talent from Dorotya after a peculiar interaction involving her ring. Notable merchants and scholars include Effie, Ghanga, and Valine.

; Mistport Fields
Mistport Fields also known as the Meadows is an area found north of Mistport where solitary farmers reside. Quests like The Ugly Little Bird, Treated Like Cattle, and Seed of Power are tied to this region.
In the eastern part of Mistport fields also lies a Lumen Paladin Bridgehead which is guarded by small group of Lumen Paladins of The Inquisitorial Concordat. This location is inhabited by merchants and scholars like Lumen Paladin Cork and Audrey here. The quests Dark Dealings in the The Tar-Trenches, The Three Altars, and A Danger to Herself and Others are relevant to this area.

; Shattered Valleys
Shattered Valleys is a farming area found east of Mistport and north of The Barrow-Necropolis. Due to Nether-Abominations incursion the area is affected by decay and other natural hazards. Here, the Archon-Ascendants can meet Almira, a self-proclaimed succubus who offers the quest Unlikely Lovers, which involves defeating the Harbinger of Doom.

; Reaper's Bluffs
Situated west of Mistport, Reaper's Bluffs is a series of steep cliffs situated above the High Seas. It is a place where renegade dwarves reside, originally led by Lohar, now affected by malevolent force that drives them to cause harm to all alongside Concordat Justiciars. Players here can meet Lagan here and engage in quests like Lost and Found, The Burning Prophet, and Keep Calm and Carrion. Beneath Reaper's Bluffs lies a large cavern known as Wrecker's Cave which served as a main hub for the dwarven rebels. This area is mostly associated with quests like The Law of the Order, Shadow Over Mistport, and A Taste of Freedom are pertinent to this sub-area.

; The Barrow-Necropolis
The Barrow-Necropolis is the graveyard found east of Mistport and north of the  itself. Run by Ryker and his servants this area hosts numerous quests, including All in the Family, A Hunger From Beyond, Stranger in a Strange Land and Speaking in Forked Tongues. It's a hub for interacting with merchants and scholars and progressing through quests on the eastern side of the map.

; The The Tar-Trenches
The The Tar-Trenches is a mining area found east of The Barrow-Necropolis. Currently overrrun by Concordat Justiciars who quarantined area in order to proceed with their secret operations. Noteworthy merchants and scholars like Gwydian Rince can be encountered here, with the main quest being On the Ropes. The The Tar-Trenches Mines are the most noteworthy location accessed via The Tar-Trenches and it is in this location that initiates face various puzzles and the Primordial Titan Aetera entombed within the Ancient Temple. Quests like A Generous Offer and Dark Dealings in the The Tar-Trenches are linked to this location.
