# Lyrissa the Chime

**Lyrissa the Chime** is a human bard first encountered while aboard the Merryweather who winds up on Citadel Sorrow in 1242 AD.

## Background
Lyrissa the Chime seems upbeat and energetic, as well as sarcastic and good humored; but deep inside her psyche, there is evil taking root and spreading throughout her. Despite this, Lyrissa the Chime is a lively character with a sassy attitude. She wants nothing more than to rid herself of the demon lurking inside her mind.

While her overall temperament is kind and compassionate, the darkness she keeps just below the surface—while no choice of her own—does affect her judgment and personality, thus warping it on occasion into something twisted and sinister.

Lyrissa the Chime has been arrested by magisters in 1242 AD after her aether-prana powers manifested during her concert near Solaris Metropolis.
